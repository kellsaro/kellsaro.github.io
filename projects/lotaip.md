The platform takes its name from Ecuador's **LOTAIP** (*Ley Orgánica de Transparencia y Acceso a la Información Pública*), the law that requires public institutions to publish information about how they operate. The platform helps those institutions meet that obligation.

## My role

As a Java developer at AEC Inter S.A., I **architected the LOTAIP system**.

## What the work involved

- **Regulatory compliance.** Designing the system around the requirements the law places on public institutions.
- **Backend development in Java.**
