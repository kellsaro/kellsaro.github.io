**alas-HIS** is a hospital management system used across healthcare institutions in Cuba. A hospital system rarely works alone: it has to exchange data with laboratories, equipment and other clinical systems.

## My role

At the Universidad de las Ciencias Informáticas I **developed backend modules** for alas-HIS, while also working as an assistant professor.

## What the work involved

- **HL7 integrations.** HL7 is the standard healthcare systems use to exchange clinical and administrative data. I implemented the integrations that let alas-HIS talk to other systems this way.
- **Backend development in Java** running on **Linux** environments.
