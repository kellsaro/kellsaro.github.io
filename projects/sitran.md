**SITRAN** is a custom ERP for **Transtur Varadero**, a tourism transportation company. It runs the core of the business: the vehicle fleet and the bookings made on it.

## My role

I developed SITRAN as a **complete project**, from the data model to the user-facing features, using **Ruby on Rails 2.2** and **PostgreSQL**.

## What the work involved

- **Fleet management.** Tracking the company's vehicles and their use.
- **Booking systems.** Managing reservations for tourism transportation services.
- **Complex business logic.** Encoding the company's own rules for how fleet and bookings fit together, which is why an off-the-shelf ERP was not enough.
